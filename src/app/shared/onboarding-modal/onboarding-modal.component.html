Choose How You Travel

Use the mode switch to change how each plate gets logged:

  • Spot (Classic): see the plate, log the point. Simple and steady.
  • Quiz (Trivia): back it up with 3 questions about the state, and earn more for each one you get right.

Keeping Score

Every stop along the route adds to your total:

Plate spotted 1-3 pts
Trivia answer 1-3 pts
Range bonus 1-8 pts

Take on the trivia and cover some ground to build the highest total in your road log.

Spotting Range

The farther you are from a state when you spot its plate, the bigger the range bonus — and a really distant find is worth more on its own too:

  • 0 - 500 miles: 1 point
  • 501 - 1000 miles: 2 points
  • 1001 - 2000 miles: 3 points, and the plate is worth 2
  • 2001 - 3000 miles: 5 points, and the plate is worth 3
  • Over 3000 miles: 8 points (a real long-hauler)
