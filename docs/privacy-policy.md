# Tag Spotter Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 25, 2026

## Overview

Tag Spotter is a mobile game for collecting state license plates, answering trivia, and earning distance bonuses.

## Data we use

- **Approximate or precise location, only when you ask us to use it**
  - The app requests location when you mark a plate as found.
  - Location is used to calculate a distance bonus for that plate.
  - If you deny location access, the plate is still recorded and the app continues to work.
- **Gameplay progress stored on your device**
  - The app stores your collection progress and point totals locally on your device.

## Data we do not currently collect from the app

- We do not currently send your location to a backend server.
- We do not currently require account creation.
- We do not currently store exact coordinates in persistent app state.

## How permissions work

- Location access is optional.
- The app asks for location only at the point of use.
- You can revoke permission later in your device settings.

## Children and sensitive data

Tag Spotter is not designed to collect sensitive personal information. Parents and guardians should review device-level permission settings for child users.
